About

I am an IT infrastructure and operations leader with deep experience in hybrid cloud, virtualization, and operational governance. I've spent 30 years keeping complex infrastructure running at scale while building the teams and processes that make it sustainable long term.

The work I'm most proud of happened during my time at First Republic Bank. We were in the middle of a major VCF and VMware Cloud on AWS modernization when the bank failed and was acquired by JPMorgan Chase in 2023, one of the largest bank failures in US history. Within days we pivoted from active modernization to full stabilization of a 100+ billion dollar institution's infrastructure. We kept the lights on, kept the team together, and then spent two years methodically decommissioning the entire environment under regulatory scrutiny, arguably the toughest thing I've had to do in my career. The senior team stayed intact through all of it. That's the thing I'm most proud of, not the technology, the people.

My leadership style is simple. I hire good engineers, give them ownership, and get out of the way. I'm honest when things are uncertain. I care more about developing the people on my team than about any platform or tool. The technical credibility matters, I stay close enough to the work to earn it, but the job is really about building a team that doesn't need you to solve every problem.

I'm currently looking for my next opportunity in IT infrastructure and operations leadership. If you're building or rebuilding an infrastructure organization and you want someone who's done it before, I'd like to talk.
